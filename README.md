
# SIDEQUEST.exe
### Reality has unfinished missions.

SIDEQUEST.exe is an open-source experiment that turns ordinary surroundings into a persistent, story-driven real-world adventure.

Your neighborhood becomes the map. Everyday objects become clues. Small explorations become chapters in a world that is uniquely yours.

> The best part of the game happens after you close the app.

## The idea

What if an AI game didn't want you to stay online?

SIDEQUEST.exe generates real-world quests that encourage observation, curiosity, and exploration. Discoveries can become clues in a fictional mythology that evolves as you explore.

## Features

- 🎯 Real-world observation quests
- 🕹️ XP, levels, and quest progression
- 📓 A browser-based discovery journal
- 🧩 The foundation for interconnected mysteries
- 💾 Local browser storage for player progress
- 🌱 Designed around offline-first and privacy-conscious principles

## Current status

**Prototype in development.**

The current browser prototype includes a quest interface, predefined randomized missions, XP tracking, and a local discovery journal.

Open-weight AI integration, vision-based discoveries, and persistent AI-generated storylines are planned features, not yet implemented.

## Open-source AI roadmap

The project aims to integrate an open-weight language model for quest generation and story continuity, plus a vision model for interpreting user-submitted observations.

The goal is to let users choose their models, run inference locally where feasible, and retain control over their discoveries.

## Principles

- **Touch grass:** The real world is the primary experience.
- **Privacy first:** Avoid collecting precise location data by default.
- **Honest AI:** Separate observed facts from fictional storytelling.
- **Open by design:** Make the architecture and model choices transparent.
- **Accessible exploration:** Missions should work on safe, public, accessible routes.
